# Dame Coffee OS roadmap audit

Audited against the production code and Supabase schema on August 10, 2026. A feature is marked complete only when its customer or staff workflow exists in the application—not because it appeared in an earlier plan.

## Status at a glance

| Original phase | Status | What exists | What remains |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 — Menu Experience | Complete with Phase 12 | Square-synced menu and pickup categories, descriptions, prices, photos, availability, featured and seasonal badges, website visibility, sold-out controls, and a private Menu Studio. Square safely owns sellable items, prices, and modifiers. | Add real item photos and descriptions as the catalog grows. |
| 3 — Event Booking | Complete | Catering estimator, address autocomplete and map, company, guests, indoor/outdoor setting, budget, notes, date and time, drink and service sliders, estimate, $200 Square deposit, Supabase request record, and full admin workflow. | Ongoing package and policy tuning only. |
| 4 — Rewards | Complete | Dame-owned accounts, points, redemptions, birthday rewards, referrals, 2× campaigns, and refund reversals. | Ongoing reward tuning only. |
| 5 — Customer Accounts | Complete | Customer profile, reward balance and activity, referrals, reward redemption, favorite drinks, signed-in pickup history, and linked catering bookings. | Optional future native reorder button. |
| 6 — Online Ordering | Complete | Square catalog, grouped menu, modifiers, cart editing, Square checkout, pickup details, confirmation, tracking, and staff status controls. | Test the live Square Register notification flow on the physical device. |
| 7 — Admin Dashboard 2.0 | Complete | Focused workspaces for revenue, orders, catering, rewards, menu, inventory, locations, events, notifications, and daily prep. | Continue polishing as later features are added. |
| 8 — Inventory | Complete | Supplies, quantities, units, low-stock limits, notes, search, filters, and low/out alerts. | Optional future automatic ingredient depletion from sales. |
| 9 — Employee Portal | Complete | Daily prep, owner/manager/barista roles, clock-in/out, schedules, today’s public and catering events, recipes, written training, and video/resource links. | Add new staff by approving their Dame OS account, then manage their role in Team. |
| 10 — Analytics | Complete | Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and yearly Square sales; selectors; drilldowns; orders; average ticket; tax; tips; discounts; service charges; top items; repeat customers; peak hour; events booked; rewards redeemed; new members; and coffee/matcha/food category views. | Ongoing reporting refinements only. |

## Completion result

The original Phase 2–10 roadmap now has an operational workflow for every promised capability. Future phases can focus on polish, physical Square device testing, automatic ingredient depletion, native reordering, and expanding staff access without replacing the current foundation.
